Malformed RSA reproduction before the final guard (7 November 2026).

The low-level parseRSAPublicKey helper accepted a SubjectPublicKeyInfo whose
RSA modulus is ASN.1 INTEGER -1. This was observed with the pre-guard library
using cabal exec / runghc; no credentials were sent.

Public invalid fixture:
-----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----
MBwwDQYJKoZIhvcNAQEBBQADCwAwCAIB/wIDAQAB
-----END PUBLIC KEY-----

Observed: negative modulus accepted by parser

The final Stage2 auth.rsa-invalid-key-structure case requires rejection before
positive-integer conversion and checks zero/negative modulus, negative/even
exponents and an unrelated algorithm identifier. Earlier benchmark/source
records are retained in stage2-documented-before-rsa-window.json.
